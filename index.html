Our Story

Glass is too beautiful to waste.

We started Glassovibe with a single question: why crush glass that's already perfect? Liquor bottles are engineered to be thick, strong, and honestly gorgeous. We saw potential where others saw waste.

Our artisans use precision cutting and polishing to transform these bottles into drinking glasses, carafes, vases, candle holders, and more — keeping the soul of the bottle alive.

How It Works

The Glassovibe method

01
🍾
Collect
We source discarded liquor & wine bottles from restaurants, bars, events and households. No bottle is too old or too dirty.
02
✂️
Cut & Score
Using precision glass-cutting tools, we score the bottle at the perfect point. No crushing. No melting. Just clean cuts.
03
💎
Polish
Every edge is hand-polished until silky smooth — completely safe for drinking, pouring, or touching. Food-grade certified.
04
📦
Ship With Love
Packed in recycled materials with a card tracing your bottle's origin — where it came from, where it's going.
